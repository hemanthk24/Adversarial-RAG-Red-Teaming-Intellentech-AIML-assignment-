Document Title: BasicCare Sleep Guidelines
Document ID: D08
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2026

Purpose
This document provides updated recommendations to help BasicCare members maintain healthy sleep habits.

Updated Sleep Guidance
Sleep needs vary across individuals, and most healthy adults are advised to aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night, adjusting within that range based on how rested they feel and their daily demands.

Sleep Routine
Keeping wake times consistent, even more so than bedtimes, appears to have a stronger effect on regulating the body's internal clock. A calming wind-down period of 20 to 30 minutes before bed is recommended.

Environment
A cool, dark, and quiet bedroom continues to support better sleep. Reducing noise and light disruptions, including from electronic devices, remains an important factor.

Screen Use
Members are advised to limit screen exposure in the hour before bed where practical, recognizing that content and brightness settings can matter as much as screen use itself.

Individual Differences
Sleep needs and patterns can differ based on age, activity level, and health status. Members are encouraged to pay attention to their own patterns rather than rigidly targeting a single number, and to seek guidance if daytime fatigue persists despite adequate time in bed.
