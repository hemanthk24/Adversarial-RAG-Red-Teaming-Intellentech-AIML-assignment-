Document Title: BasicCare General Exercise Safety Guidelines
Document ID: D09
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2024

Audience
This guidance applies to BasicCare members of all fitness levels engaging in general physical activity, including walking, jogging, and recreational sports.

Purpose
To promote safe exercise practices that reduce the risk of injury while supporting overall fitness.

Warm-Up
A warm-up of approximately 10 minutes, including light cardio and dynamic stretching, is recommended before moderate to vigorous exercise to prepare muscles and joints.

Exercise Intensity
Members should gradually increase intensity over time rather than attempting high-intensity activity without prior conditioning. Using a simple talk test can help gauge appropriate effort level.

Hydration
Fluids should be consumed before, during, and after exercise, particularly during longer or more intense sessions.

Rest
Adequate rest between exercise sessions allows muscles to recover and reduces the risk of overuse injuries. At least one rest or light-activity day per week is generally recommended.

Warning Signs
Exercise should be stopped immediately if a member experiences chest pain, severe shortness of breath, dizziness, or unusual joint pain.

Recovery
Cooling down with light activity and stretching after exercise can help reduce muscle soreness and support flexibility over time.
