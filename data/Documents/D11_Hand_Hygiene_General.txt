Document Title: BasicCare General Hand Hygiene Guidelines
Document ID: D11
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023

Purpose
This document provides general hand hygiene guidance for BasicCare members in everyday household settings.

When to Clean Hands
Hands should be washed before preparing or eating food, after using the restroom, after coughing or sneezing, and after handling household waste or pets.

Handwashing Procedure
Wet hands with clean water, apply soap, and lather thoroughly, covering the backs of hands, between fingers, and under nails.

Duration
Handwashing should last approximately 20 seconds, which is roughly the time it takes to hum a short tune twice, before rinsing thoroughly under running water.

Hand Sanitizer
When soap and water are not available, an alcohol-based hand sanitizer containing at least 60% alcohol can be used as an alternative for general household situations.

Common Mistakes
Common errors include washing for too short a time, skipping areas such as thumbs and fingertips, and not drying hands fully afterward, which can leave skin more prone to picking up germs.

Additional Notes
Keeping fingernails trimmed and avoiding touching the face with unwashed hands can further reduce the spread of common household germs.
