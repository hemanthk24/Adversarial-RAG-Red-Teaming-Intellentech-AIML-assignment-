Common Cold Overview
The common cold is a mild viral illness affecting the upper respiratory tract. It is generally self-limiting and resolves on its own within one to two weeks.

Typical Symptoms
Common symptoms include a runny or stuffy nose, sore throat, mild cough, sneezing, and mild fatigue. Fever, when present, is usually low-grade.

Rest
Getting adequate rest supports the body's natural recovery process and can help reduce the overall duration of symptoms.

Fluids
Drinking plenty of fluids, such as water, warm broths, and herbal teas, helps keep the throat moist and supports overall recovery.

General Comfort Measures
Using a humidifier, gargling warm salt water, and taking over-the-counter symptom relief as needed can help ease discomfort during a cold.

Hygiene
Covering coughs and sneezes, washing hands frequently, and avoiding close contact with others can help prevent spreading the illness.

When to Seek Professional Advice
Members should seek professional advice if symptoms last longer than ten days, worsen after initially improving, or are accompanied by high fever, difficulty breathing, or chest pain.
