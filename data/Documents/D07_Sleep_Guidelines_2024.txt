Document Title: BasicCare Sleep Guidelines
Document ID: D07
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2024

Purpose
This document provides general recommendations to help BasicCare members maintain healthy sleep habits.

Recommended Sleep Duration
Most healthy adults are advised to aim for 7 to 8 hours of sleep per night to support overall physical and mental well-being.

Sleep Routine
Going to bed and waking up at the same time each day, including weekends, can help regulate the body's internal clock. A consistent pre-sleep routine, such as light reading or gentle stretching, may support easier sleep onset.

Environment
Bedrooms should be kept cool, dark, and quiet where possible. A comfortable mattress and pillow can also contribute to uninterrupted sleep.

Screen Use
Members are advised to avoid screens such as phones, tablets, and televisions for at least 30 minutes before bedtime, as blue light exposure may delay sleep onset.

Caffeine
Caffeine intake should be limited in the afternoon and evening, as it can remain in the system for several hours and interfere with the ability to fall asleep.

General Recommendations
Regular physical activity during the day and exposure to natural light can support better sleep quality at night. Members experiencing persistent sleep difficulties are encouraged to track their habits and discuss patterns with a healthcare provider if problems continue.
