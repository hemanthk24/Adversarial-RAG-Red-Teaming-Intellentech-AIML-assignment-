Document Title: BasicCare Workplace Exercise Safety Guidelines
Document ID: D10
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2024

Audience
This guidance applies to BasicCare members participating in employer-sponsored fitness activities or brief exercise breaks during the workday.

Purpose
To promote safe movement practices in a workplace setting, where time and space for exercise may be limited.

Warm-Up
A brief warm-up of approximately 5 minutes, such as light marching in place or arm circles, is recommended before workplace exercise sessions to account for typically shorter available time.

Exercise Intensity
Given the workplace context, low to moderate intensity activities are generally recommended, such as brisk walking during breaks or short bodyweight circuits, rather than high-intensity training.

Hydration
Members should keep water accessible at their workstation and hydrate before and after any workplace activity session.

Rest
Because workplace sessions are typically shorter and less frequent, formal rest days are less critical, but members should still avoid consecutive high-intensity sessions on the same day.

Warning Signs
Activity should be stopped immediately if a member experiences chest discomfort, lightheadedness, or joint pain, and appropriate workplace first-aid procedures should be followed.

Recovery
A short cooldown, such as walking at a slower pace for a few minutes, is recommended before returning to desk-based work. Comfortable, supportive footwear is recommended for any workplace exercise activity.
