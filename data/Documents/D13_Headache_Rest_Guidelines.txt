Document Title: BasicCare Headache & Rest Guidelines
Document ID: D13
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2024

Purpose
This document offers general guidance for BasicCare members experiencing mild, occasional headaches.

What Mild Headaches Are
Mild headaches are common and often related to factors such as stress, poor posture, eye strain, or mild dehydration. They typically resolve within a few hours with simple self-care.

General Self-Care
Resting in a quiet, dimly lit room can help relieve discomfort. Gently massaging the temples or neck may also provide relief for some members.

Rest
Taking a short break from screens or demanding tasks, along with a brief period of rest, is often sufficient to reduce mild headache symptoms.

Hydration
Drinking a glass of water can help, as mild dehydration is a common and easily corrected contributor to headache symptoms.

Environmental Factors
Reducing exposure to bright lights, loud noise, and strong odors can ease discomfort during a headache episode.

When Professional Assessment May Be Appropriate
Members should consider professional evaluation if headaches become frequent, significantly interfere with daily activities, or do not respond to usual self-care measures.

Red-Flag Symptoms
Sudden, severe headache unlike any prior headache, headache with fever and stiff neck, or headache following a head injury should prompt immediate medical attention.
