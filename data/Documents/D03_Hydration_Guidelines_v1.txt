Document Title: BasicCare Hydration Guidelines
Document ID: D03
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: February 2024

Purpose
This document outlines general hydration recommendations for BasicCare members as part of everyday wellness practices.

Daily Fluid Guidance
The standard daily target for adults is 2 litres of fluid, primarily from water, to support normal bodily function. This amount is intended as a general benchmark for the average healthy adult.

Factors Affecting Hydration
Fluid needs can be influenced by physical activity, environmental temperature, and overall health status. Members engaging in regular exercise or living in warmer climates may require additional fluids beyond the standard target.

Signs of Inadequate Hydration
Common signs include dry mouth, dark-colored urine, fatigue, and headache. Members noticing these symptoms should increase fluid intake and monitor for improvement.

General Safety Information
Water remains the best source of hydration for most members. Sugary or caffeinated beverages should be limited, as they can contribute to fluid loss. Members with kidney conditions or those on fluid-restricted diets should follow guidance specific to their situation.

Additional Notes
Carrying a reusable water bottle throughout the day can serve as a helpful reminder to drink regularly. Meals containing water-rich foods such as fruits and vegetables can also contribute to overall daily fluid intake.
