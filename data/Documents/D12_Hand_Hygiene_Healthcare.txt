Document Title: BasicCare Clinic Hand Hygiene Guidelines
Document ID: D12
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2023

Purpose
This document provides hand hygiene guidance for BasicCare clinic staff and volunteers in healthcare and workplace clinical settings.

When to Clean Hands
Hands must be cleaned before and after any contact with a patient, before performing any clean or aseptic task, after exposure to bodily fluids, and after contact with surfaces near a patient.

Handwashing Procedure
Wet hands, apply soap or an approved antiseptic solution, and lather thoroughly, ensuring coverage of wrists, backs of hands, between fingers, and under nails, following the facility's approved technique.

Duration
In clinical settings, handwashing should last approximately 40 to 60 seconds to ensure adequate reduction of transient microorganisms, given the higher risk of exposure to pathogens.

Hand Sanitizer
An alcohol-based hand rub containing at least 70% alcohol is recommended between patient contacts in the clinic setting when hands are not visibly soiled, applied for at least 20 seconds until dry.

Common Mistakes
Common errors in clinical settings include rushing through the required duration, failing to remove jewelry that can harbor microorganisms, and not performing hand hygiene between different patients or tasks.

Additional Notes
Staff should follow posted clinic protocols and report any skin irritation from frequent handwashing to their supervisor, as alternative products may be available.
