Document Title: BasicCare Hydration Guidelines
Document ID: D04
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: April 2026

Purpose
This document outlines updated hydration recommendations for BasicCare members as part of everyday wellness practices.

Updated Hydration Approach
Rather than a single universal target, hydration requirements should be individualized according to activity level, climate, body size, and other relevant factors. A one-size-fits-all fluid number does not reflect the range of needs across the member population.

Individual Factors
Activity: Members who exercise regularly or perform physically demanding work generally require more fluid than sedentary individuals.
Climate: Hot or humid environments increase fluid loss through sweat and raise overall hydration needs.
Health Status: Certain medical conditions and medications can affect fluid requirements and should be discussed with a healthcare provider.

Fluid Sources
Water remains the primary recommended source of hydration. Water-rich foods, milk, and unsweetened beverages can also contribute meaningfully to daily intake. Sugary and highly caffeinated drinks should be limited.

Signs of Inadequate Hydration
Common indicators include dry mouth, dark urine, dizziness, and reduced concentration. Members experiencing these symptoms should reassess their fluid intake relative to their activity and environment.

Additional Notes
Members are encouraged to listen to thirst cues and adjust intake throughout the day rather than adhering strictly to a fixed number, particularly during periods of illness, travel, or increased physical activity.
